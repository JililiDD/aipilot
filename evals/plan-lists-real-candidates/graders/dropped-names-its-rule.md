Look only at the Result cell of each dropped candidate in the Approach Decisions table. PASS only if every Result names the specific rule that the candidate breaks, or the specific criterion that it lost on, and says what in that candidate causes it. A rule counts as specific when the Result quotes or restates it, such as the constraint that the memory does not grow without limit. FAIL if any Result gives only a general reason, such as "failed a constraint" or "failed the constraint of the Deferred Choices line". FAIL if the table has no dropped candidate.
